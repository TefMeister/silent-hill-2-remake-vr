About UEVR in this package


This package includes UEVR, made by praydog, in the AFW build made by PureDark
(UEVR AFW v1.0-beta.6, unchanged, exactly as downloaded from https://github.com/PureDark/UEVR/releases/tag/UEVR_AFW_v1.0-beta.6).

UEVR's own licence reads "Copyright (c) 2022-2025 praydog. All rights reserved." We do not own UEVR and we do not
claim to. We asked praydog whether we could include it in this mod and have had no answer, so we include it openly,
named and credited, because the mod is built around this exact build and does not work with any other.

If praydog or PureDark asks us to stop, we will take UEVR out of the package straight away and point to their own
downloads instead. Please open an issue on https://github.com/TefMeister/silent-hill-2-remake-vr if you are one of
them, or if you know of a problem with this.

UEVR: https://github.com/praydog/UEVR
UEVR AFW build: https://github.com/PureDark/UEVR

The files UEVRInjector.exe, UEVRInjector.pdb and UEVRInjector.dll.config are left out: the mod's own launcher
(SH2VR.exe) starts the game and loads UEVR itself, following the steps of praydog's uevr-frontend (MIT licence,
LICENSE-uevr-frontend.txt).
