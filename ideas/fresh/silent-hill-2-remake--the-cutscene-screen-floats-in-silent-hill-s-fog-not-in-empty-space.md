# The cutscene screen floats in Silent Hill's fog, not in empty space

> "cutscene enviromemt to have the fog around me" — 2026-11-13 (idea 2)

While a cutscene plays on the big screen, what is around you should be the town's fog, so the screen hangs in the mist
instead of in a plain backdrop.

**What it'd take (unchecked):** two possible routes. Keep the real world drawn around the screen (where the player
stands, the game's own fog included), which costs frame rate during cutscenes; or a cheap stand-in, a fog-coloured
shell around the viewer that drifts slowly. Which one fits depends on what the screen's surroundings are made of today,
not yet looked at.
