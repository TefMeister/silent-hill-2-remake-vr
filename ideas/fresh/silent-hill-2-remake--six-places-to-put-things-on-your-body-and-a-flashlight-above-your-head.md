**No menu. Everything lives somewhere on you, and you reach for it.**

| where | what | hand |
| --- | --- | --- |
| right hip | pistol | right grip |
| left hip | map | right grip |
| right shoulder | shotgun | right grip |
| left shoulder | rifle | right grip |
| left hip | ammo pouch | **left** grip |
| **above the head, dead centre** | flashlight | **left** grip — to stow it *and* to hold it |

**⭐ The two details that make this a design and not a list.** Both hips are used *twice* — right
grip at the left hip takes the map, left grip at the same spot takes ammo — so which hand you reach
with decides what you get, and the two never collide. And the flashlight lives **above your head**
rather than on your body: in a game whose whole atmosphere is a torch beam in fog, putting the light
overhead means stowing it still lights the room in front of you, and taking it means you chose to aim
it. That is a comfort decision disguised as a holster.

**What it'd take:** this is the same family of work as the Resident Evil hip pouches already filed on
this account, so the thinking is not new — but the mechanism is completely different, because there
the game was reverse engineered and here a general injector does the hard part. In UEVR terms it
means knowing where the player's head and hands are (it does), defining points relative to the body
(the normal way to do this), and telling the game to swap weapon and use an item (**the unchecked
part — whether those actions can be reached at all from a profile in this game**).
⚠️ Nothing here has been tried; the game has never been launched on this account.
